FAA registration, aircraft and registered owner from the FAA releasable registry (official). OpenSky fills in only where FAA has no match: crowdsourced, unverified, sometimes stale. None means neither source identifies the address.

Types are ICAO Doc 8643 designators (OpenSky, or derived from the FAA model). Wide-body means heavy wake category; other non-airliner jets count as business jets. "Airline" is a heuristic from callsign, owner and type; registered owner is not necessarily the operator.

First and last seen are reception times; max altitude is barometric, in feet. Read-only view served from the Sky Scanner database.
